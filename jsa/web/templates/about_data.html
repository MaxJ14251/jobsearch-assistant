About this data

Where the education figures on job pages and Turbo cards come from, and what they can and can't tell you.

This role, nationwide

The line "This role, nationwide" shows the highest education held by workers 25 and older in the occupation a posting's title matches. It is what people in that kind of job hold, nationwide; not what the job requires, and not who this company hires.

{% if sources.census %}
  • Education by occupation: Statistics Canada, table 98-10-0449-01, highest certificate, diploma or degree by occupation, Census of Population {{ year }}: employed workers 25 and older. Trades certificates, college and CEGEP diplomas and university certificates below a bachelor's are counted together; there is no "some college, no credential" group, so those people count under high school.
  • Job titles to occupations: the National Occupational Classification (NOC) 2021 Version 1.0 class titles and index of titles, mapped to census occupations; a few titles postings use differently are set by hand.
  • Adapted from Statistics Canada, Table 98-10-0449-01 and NOC 2021 Version 1.0, used under the Statistics Canada Open Licence. This does not constitute an endorsement by Statistics Canada of this product.
{% else %}
  • Education by occupation: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Employment Projections, table 5.3, Educational attainment for workers 25 years and older by detailed occupation, from the American Community Survey, {{ year }}. Public US-government data.

A title is matched word for word, or on its words when at least {{ (min_confidence * 100)|round|int }}% of them are shared and the last word (the occupation) is the same. Otherwise nothing is shown. The occupation it matched is always named, so a poor match can be seen.
