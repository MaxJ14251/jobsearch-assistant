About this data

Where the education figures on job pages and Turbo cards come from, and what they can and can't tell you.

This role, nationwide

The line "This role, nationwide" shows the highest education held by workers 25 and older in the occupation a posting's title matches. It is what people in that kind of job hold, nationwide; not what the job requires, and not who this company hires.

  • Education by occupation: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Employment Projections, table 5.3, Educational attainment for workers 25 years and older by detailed occupation, from the American Community Survey, {{ year }}. Public US-government data.
  • Job titles to occupations: This page includes information from the O*NET 31.0 Database by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA). Used under the CC BY 4.0 license. This tool has modified all or some of this information: titles were normalized and mapped to BLS occupation codes. A title is matched word for word, or on its words when at least {{ (min_confidence * 100)|round|int }}% of them are shared and the last word (the occupation) is the same. Otherwise nothing is shown. The occupation it matched is always named, so a poor match can be seen.

What postings ask for

"This posting asks for" and the Companies page read each posting's own text, with no model. {{ degree_caveat }} They cover the postings in your tracker, which are the ones that matched your search.
